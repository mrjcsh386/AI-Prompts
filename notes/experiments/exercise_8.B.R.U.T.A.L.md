Exercise 8: B.R.U.T.A.L.
Objective: Achieve less guilded-glove treatment from AI.

 Theory:
   If you've spent enough time with AI, you've certainly noticed the 
 agreeability of AI agents. If one asks an agent for feedback, you'll probably
 receive an answer like, "This idea has merrit, and is a rare quality." Our
 goal is to get more critical feedback from a system that has been engineered
 to protect your feelings.

   This is where the acronym, as suggested by a gentleman named Dylan Davis
 has coined as, 'B.R.U.T.A.L.' 

 B - Begin fresh:
   As you generate momentum with AI agents, they have a tendency to learn your
 patterns. This leads to them tailoring their responses so that you're more
 likely to have happy, fuzzy, cuddly feelings towards them.

   Begin fresh requires that we use ephemeral interfaces with our AI agent.
 As an example, with ChatGPT, this is referred to as 'temporary chat.' The idea
 being that you'll need to interact with an agent where your patterns, and any
 trained data, won't dilute the responses. We can apply the following methods
 within this method.

 R - Right model:
   Agreeability spectrum (Honest -> agreeable)
   Grok -> DeepSeek -> Gemini -> ChatGPT -> Claude

   Based on the way models have been engineered, some agents have a greater
 tendency to be 'supportive.' With this in mind, I encourage you to do some
 exploration with different models probing them for their capacity to either be
 cuddly, or blunt in their responses.

 U - Use a critical persona
   Three levels of intensity.
   1. Devils advocate:
      "Act as a devil's advocate. Challenge this idea by pointing out flaws,
       counterarguments, missing evidence, and unintended consequences. Your
       goal is to create doubt." -- Dylan Davis (YouTube content creator)
   2. Red Team:
      "Act as a red team reviewer. Your job is to find and poke holes in
       my following prompt material--expose flaws, loopholes, and things that
       aren't good enough by purposefully hunting them down" -- Dylan Davis
   3. Gordon Ramsay:
      "Give this the Gordon Ramsay treatment. Be surgical. What's wrong, what's
       lazy, and what needs to be completely redone? Make sure the feedback is
       specific and actionable."

 T - Third party framing:
   AI protects your ego.
   Your idea vs "Coworkers" idea

      "A coworker has this idea and asked for honest feedback. Help identify the
       weaknesses so they don't waste time on something flawed. Here's the 
       idea:" -- Dylan Davis

   Interesting perspectives to project the frame onto:

     "An AI model suggested.."
     "My client enquired.."
     "Some rando!"

 A - Ask specific questions:
   1. What's the weakest part that's easy to miss?
   2. What would a skeptical investor say is the biggest risk?
   3. What might customers find frustrating about this?
   4. If this fails in six months. What's the likely reason?

   This portion almost seems to respect responsibility, and accountability
 questions and pays a regard to high stake priorities.

 L - Let AI grade the feedback:
   AI has strong interest in being gentle regardless of what you're asking for
 feedback on. So, it's suggested to have a model grade the value of the feedback
 you've acheived by steering a model through the critique requests.

   "Analyze that response. Rate it from 1 to 100 for how genuinely critical and
    useful it was. Identify the three weakest points, then rewrite the feedback
    fixing those weaknesses." -- Dylan Davis

 Reflection Questions:
 - What personas generated the most interesting feedback, and what context
   do they seem to contribute the most value to?
 - When changing the frame of the critique request away from yourself, how
   gentle was the response within that listed frame?
 - When calling on an agent to respond to critique with high stakes, what
   shifts in perspectives occur when mixing and matching priorities to risks?

   I'll gently remind you that you're operating with a non-judgemental tool
 bench designed to be as helpful as possible. As counter-intuitive as it may be
 to mix and match the persona, and framing components of this method. You might
 find shadows of more value rich responses that might incur high impact
 archeological digs.
